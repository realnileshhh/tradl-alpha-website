## 1 · THE ONE-PARAGRAPH BRAND

Tradl AI is the agentic trading platform for India's serious traders. It fuses language models that read a trader's intent with a deterministic quant engine that computes the answer on live market data. Every output is a computed fact the trader can read, re-run, and audit. Every call the intelligence makes is numbered, dated, and graded in public, including the misses. Tradl does not predict, does not tip, and does not recommend. It computes.

## 3 · THE NARRATIVE SPINE (the story the website tells)

### 3.1 The four waves
Indian markets have moved in waves, each unlocked by infrastructure:
- **Wave 1 · Access (1990s–2000s):** physical to digital. Markets reach retail for the first time.
- **Wave 2 · Cost (2000s–2010s):** discount broking. Participation widens.
- **Wave 3 · Convenience (2010s–2025):** the India stack. An account in minutes, money in seconds.
- **Wave 4 · Intelligence (2025+):** AI becomes the architecture of trading itself. This is Tradl's wave.

Website usage rule: the waves frame is a *manifesto/story* device, not a homepage hero device. It earns depth on the manifesto page; the homepage leads with product truth.

### 3.2 The digital-skill thesis
Trading is a digital skill. Every digital skill runs on the same chain:
**Intent → Insight → Analysis → Execution.**
AI has already collapsed this chain in coding (Cursor, Claude) and in creative work (Sora, Veo). Trading is next, and it has resisted until now for one reason: LLMs are probabilistic and trading is numerical. A hallucinated Greeks value is worse than no answer.

### 3.3 The architecture answer
Tradl's dual foundation: **LLM reads intent and plans. Quant computes it in transparent code on live NSE/BSE data.** No hallucinations. Glass box. Reproducible. This is the technical spine behind the mantra:

> **"We compute, we don't predict."**

This line is tagline-grade. It appears once per page maximum, always verbatim, never paraphrased.

### 3.4 The falsifiability posture
The brand's deepest trust move: calls are frozen before the open, graded after, and the misses stay on the page. Morning Decode's public scorecard is the flagship proof. Nothing on the website says "accurate" or "reliable"; everything shows a record.

## 5 · BRAND PILLARS (the four things every page must make felt)

1. **Determinism.** Intelligence you can audit. Prompt in, generated code visible, computed result out. The Playground is this pillar made physical.
2. **Falsifiability.** Records over adjectives. Fired counts, resolution rates, graded archives, misses kept. If a claim has no number and date, it doesn't ship.
3. **Embedded intelligence.** The intelligence lives on the trader's existing surfaces: the chart, the chain, the stock page, the screen. Never "come chat with our bot."
4. **Depth first.** Built for the trader who already works hard at this. Density, keyboard-first ambitions, pro vocabulary used correctly, zero dumbing-down. Premium is earned through depth, not price framing.

## 6 · AUDIENCE (condensed for site decisions)

**Primary: the Semi-Pro / Aspiring trader.** 2+ years in, self-taught, swing/momentum, runs a Scan → Chart → Signals → Chain → Order workflow across four disconnected apps. Core pain: continuity. Loses entries to cross-verification. Wants to become systematic. This is the alpha's volume signup.

**Secondary: the Pro trader.** Systematic, rules-based, ₹1 Cr+ turnover. Core pain: integration; surfaces that don't know each other exist. Hardest to impress, highest halo value. The site's density and honesty register are calibrated to survive this reader's scrutiny. If the page reads credible to a pro, it reads aspirational to a semi-pro.

**Explicit non-target at alpha:** tip-seekers and casual scalpers. The site should feel slightly over their heads by design. Copy never mentions tips except to renounce them.

## 7 · VOICE & TONE

**Voice in one line:** an instrument, not a salesperson. Terse, matter-of-fact, numbers-forward, quietly confident.

**Register rules:**
- Headlines pair a plain statement with a payoff span: "Others guess. *We compute.*"
- Body is sentence case, short declaratives. Labels are UPPERCASE. Titles are Title Case.
- Numbers carry the emotion. "Fired 14 times, 9 resolved up in 10 sessions" beats any adjective.
- Confident contrasts are the signature rhetorical move: "Computed, not guessed." "A record, not a promise." "Built into the chart, not bolted onto a chatbot."
- ₹ symbol always (never "Rs"); Indian units (lakh, Cr); no USD on the trader-facing site.
- One idea per sentence. If a sentence has "and" twice, split it.
- Warmth is allowed exactly one place per page: a lowercase serif-italic beat (e.g., "the market, finally *intelligent*."). Everywhere else stays instrument-grade.

**Hard bans (customer-facing copy linter):**
- No em-dashes or double hyphens in any customer-facing string.
- No emoji anywhere in design or site copy.
- No exclamation marks.
- Banned words: reliable, guaranteed, accurate (as a bare adjective), target (price context), buy, sell, recommendation, tip, sure-shot, multibagger, alpha (as returns-promise; "alpha" as release stage is fine), beat the market, get rich.
- No superlatives without a number attached ("the fastest" is banned; "1.9s on a 3,000-stock scan" ships).

## 8 · COMPLIANCE RAILS (bind every page)

- SEBI Research Analyst perimeter: computed and historical analytics only. Never recommendations, price targets, fair values, or buy/sell language. The RA registration number sits in the footer sitewide.
- The "share price target" search intent is served only via implied expectations (reverse-DCF framing: "At ₹X, the market is pricing in ~Y% revenue CAGR"). Never a target.
- Data attribution (CMOTS, NSE, BSE) in the footer and on data-bearing modules.
- Everything AI-derived carries the ◈ mark and a basis line (what was checked, what was computed).
- Absent data is a stated fact, never a blank or a placeholder: "Not in the F&O segment." "0.00% · Never paid."
- No broking, license, or execution claims anywhere (per founder decision, Aug 2026).
- Risk disclosure line in the footer: markets are subject to risk; Tradl publishes computed analytics, not advice.

## 11 · BRAND TENSIONS · NAMED, NOT SMOOTHED

1. **Premium vs. free alpha.** The site sells a premium-instrument feeling while the product is free and open. Resolution: scarcity lives in *access tiers of tools* (Private Access badges on Pattern Sniper, Smart Chain, Strategy Copilot), not in price. Free is framed as "alpha stage," never "free forever."
2. **Agentic ambition vs. shipped assistance.** The category word is "agentic"; the alpha ships assistance. Resolution: the site says agentic as *direction* ("the road to agentic trading") and shows assistance as *shipped fact*. No screenshots of unshipped autonomy.
3. **Depth positioning vs. conversion pressure.** Density can suppress signups. Resolution: the conversion architecture (doc 03, §2) does the converting; the brand never cheapens itself with urgency banners, countdowns, or popups.
4. **"Edge" naming vs. RA perimeter.** "Edge" could be read as promised returns. Resolution: Edge is always framed as workflow edge (speed, integration, verification), with a proof element adjacent; never paired with returns language.
